Business insights

    How the metrics are calculated
    • Total sales = sum of the sales amount of every order in the filtered data.
    • Total orders = number of order rows in the filtered data (one row is one order of one product).
    • Average order value (AOV) = Total sales / Total orders.
    • Sales growth = (Sales in the selected period - Sales in the previous period of the same length) / Sales in the previous period. It shows n/a when no earlier period exists or the previous period has no sales.

    How I think about data

    Dashboards are not the goal. Better decisions are.

    A report is only useful when someone acts on it. This is the checklist I follow on every project.

    1. Understand the questionStart from the decision, not the dataset.
    2. Clean before you chartTrust comes from tidy, consistent data.
    3. Model it simplyA clear star schema makes every measure easier.
    4. Design for the readerOne page, one story, no clutter.
    5. Check what changedFollow up and measure the result.
